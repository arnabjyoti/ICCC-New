Automated Anti-Theft & Audit Protocol
Active 24/7
Step 1
GPS Ground Distance Verification Every submitted fuel bill traces the vehicle's actual movement route in travel_histories to calculate ground kilometers traveled since the previous refill.
Step 2
Dynamic Mileage Reconciliation Ground distance is divided by liters claimed to derive actual real-world km/L. If efficiency drops below 30% of benchmark, an instant theft alarm triggers.
Step 3
Tank Capacity Overflow Guard Automatically rejects or flags inflated fake receipts where claimed liters exceed the vehicle's physical fuel tank capacity.
Fleet Calibration Benchmarks
  • Standard Bus Benchmark Mileage 4.50 km / Liter
  • Standard Compactor / Truck Tank 150.00 Liters
  • Theft Alert Threshold < 3.15 km/L (> 30% Drop)
  • Diesel Price Index ₹92.50 / Liter
Tank sizes and mileage standards can be adjusted per vehicle in the Calibration tab.
